About Our Ganesh Youth

Our Bappa ❤️

Our Ganesh Utsav is a celebration organized with devotion, unity and the active participation of our youth.

Every year, we come together to welcome Lord Ganesha and celebrate the festival with pooja, cultural programs, decorations and community activities.

Our goal is to spread happiness, maintain unity among our community and celebrate the festival together.
